## Overview

For Off-Road Illini's 2025-26 car, I designed a dog-clutch engagement for the gearbox, along with the shift fork that moves it and a case cap. The design went through several dated iterations (Nov 2025 through spring 2026) before being released as manufacturing drawings in December 2025.

## Design

**Driving dog gear (output-side clutch claw).** A 6061 aluminum part, 3.15 in long with a Ø1.75 in claw flange. It has three engagement claws spaced at 120°, each defined within 40° of the circle. All inside edges of the claws are filleted (R.1) to reduce stress concentrations, and the claw tops get an R.02 edge break.

**Shift fork.** A 6061 aluminum fork, 3.2 in long, with a 1.5 in jaw opening that rides in the dog gear's groove. It has two Ø.20 through-holes for mounting to the shift rod.
